In the last 3 months, an additional 892 megawatts (MW) of solar across 4,768 projects have been energized in the State of Illinois, bringing the total to 6,557 MW. Despite the expiration of the 30% Residential Clean Energy Tax Credit in 2025, solar projects continue to be deployed across the state at a record pace.
