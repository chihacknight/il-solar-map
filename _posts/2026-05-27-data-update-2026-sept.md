In the last 4 months, an additional 783 megawatts (MW) of solar across 6,605 projects have been energized in the State of Illinois, bringing the total to 7,340 MW. Despite the expiration of the 30% Residential Clean Energy Tax Credit in 2025, solar projects continue to be deployed across the state at a record pace.
